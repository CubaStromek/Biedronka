BIEDRONKA - Shopping Data Viewer
=================================

Version: 1.2.0

ABOUT THIS APPLICATION
----------------------
Biedronka is a desktop application for managing and analyzing
shopping data from the Polish Biedronka grocery store chain.

Main features:
- Import JSON receipts from Biedronka
- Automatic PLN to CZK conversion using CNB exchange rates
- Product category management
- Product price history tracking
- Multi-file upload support
